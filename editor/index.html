Claude

When on, Claude can read and change the sliders, switch photos and export. To judge an edit it is sent a small preview of the open photo.

Library

This browser may clear stored photos and edits after a long break or if its website data is cleared. Back up your edits now and then; with the backup and your original photo files you can get everything back.
