Where do my key and photos go?
  • Your key stays in this browser. CanvasShutter has no server, so its makers never see your key, your photos or your chat.
  • The key is forgotten when you close this tab, unless you tick "Stay connected on this device". Then it is kept in this browser, where anyone using this browser could use it.
  • When the assistant needs to look at your photo, a small copy (about 1000 pixels wide) goes straight from your browser to the AI provider you connected, together with your messages, file names and slider settings. Your original photos never leave this device.
  • You pay your AI provider for what you use. A key with a spending limit is a good idea. Disconnect removes the key from this browser.
